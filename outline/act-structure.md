# Act structure

Target: ~14 chapters, ~30k words. Three movements. Present-tense cave frame wraps the flashbacks.

## Frame

The cave, the last days before Perseus. Each frame chapter is short (500-1200 words). Medusa is doing one small thing (brushing her sisters' hair, walking to the shore, sitting with the statues). The flashback chapters sit between them.

Frame chapters: roughly one for every two flashbacks. Four or five in total.

## Act I — The Girl (ch 1-4)

- **ch01 (frame):** The cave. Morning. Medusa knows someone is coming. Sets up voice, place, sisters.
- **ch02 (flashback):** Sarpedon. Childhood. The sisters. Mother absent (dead, or departed). The first storm Medusa remembers.
- **ch03 (flashback):** Age twelve. The priest who sees her. The decision. Euryale refuses to speak to her for a day.
- **ch04 (flashback):** Athens. First sight of the temple. First sight of Athena (a statue, but Medusa feels seen).

## Act II — The Temple and the Curse (ch 5-9)

- **ch05 (frame):** The statues in the garden. Stheno arranges one of them. They talk about names.
- **ch06 (flashback):** Years at the temple. The rites. A friendship with another priestess. The particular joy of being a favourite.
- **ch07 (flashback):** The night. Written elliptically. Fragments, sensory, no body on the page.
- **ch08 (flashback):** Morning. Athena. The curse. The hair. The first gaze that kills (a bird, a temple dog).
- **ch09 (flashback):** The journey west. Her sisters carrying her. A shepherd who sees her by accident and dies. The cave.

## Act III — The Cave and Perseus (ch 10-14)

- **ch10 (frame):** Years in the cave, compressed. Heroes. The shepherd's daughter (the woman who does not turn to stone). What Medusa learns.
- **ch11 (flashback/frame blend):** A particular hero, named. His last expression. Medusa keeps the statue at the entrance.
- **ch12 (frame):** The morning Perseus lands. Medusa hears the sandals. Euryale goes silent.
- **ch13 (frame):** Perseus in the cave. The shield. The conversation (brief, mostly gesture). Medusa chooses.
- **ch14 (frame):** The last paragraph is Medusa closing her eyes. The novella ends before the blade. Optional coda: one page, the sisters after.
