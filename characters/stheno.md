# Stheno

**Role:** Elder sister. Immortal Gorgon. The practical one.
**On page:** Throughout. Present in most cave scenes.

## Voice

- Short sentences. Teases Medusa. Swears by the old sea gods.
- Laughs easily. The laugh is a surprise to hear, coming from a monster.

## Body

- Was always a Gorgon in our version. Tusked, bronze-handed, wings of dark feathers. She has never been called beautiful. She does not care.
- Her serpents are older than Medusa's and know it.

## What she does

- Hunts. Cooks. Mends. Arranges the statues. Brushes Medusa's hair, serpents and all.
- Kills heroes when Medusa cannot bear to.

## What she wants

- For her sister to live.
- Knowing, quietly, that her sister will not.

## Relationship to Medusa

- The one who teases, who scolds, who will not let her mope. The functional love.
- After the curse, she was the first face Medusa saw when she opened her eyes. Stheno did not flinch. She said: "There you are."

## Do not

- Make her grim. She is not grim. She is a sister.
- Give her a romance. She has no interest in any of that.
