# Euryale

**Role:** Elder sister. Immortal Gorgon. The one who sings.
**On page:** Throughout. The emotional centre of the cave.

## Voice

- Longer sentences than Stheno. Old-fashioned phrases. Sings old songs, including songs from before the Olympians.
- When she is frightened for Medusa, she goes silent. This is the tell.

## Body

- Gorgon. Her wings are longer and paler than Stheno's. Her voice, unaltered by the serpents in her hair, is the most human thing about her.

## What she does

- Sings. Cleans. Tells Medusa the old stories. Remembers their mother, whom Medusa does not.
- Has never killed a man. Will not. Stheno handles that.

## What she wants

- To keep Medusa alive as long as she can.
- To be, when the time comes, the one holding Medusa's hand.

## Relationship to Medusa

- The softer sister. Was Medusa's second mother.
- Mourns the girl Medusa was, more visibly than Medusa will allow herself to.
- At the end, she is the one Medusa looks for first when she hears Perseus on the shore.

## Do not

- Make her weak. Grief is not weakness.
- Let her talk Medusa out of her choice at the end. She understands.
