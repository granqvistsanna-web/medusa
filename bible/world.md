# World

## Setting

A mythic Hellenic world contemporaneous with the heroic age of Perseus. Bronze, not iron. Olive oil, wine, barley, wool. No coinage of the classical sort — weights of metal, barter, xenia (guest-friendship) as an economic force.

## Cosmology

Three realms:

- **The mortal world.** Greece and its islands, the Aegean, the edges where myth bleeds into geography (the far west, beyond the Pillars; the Hyperborean north; the Ethiopian south).
- **Olympus.** Present but distant. Gods descend; mortals do not ascend without invitation.
- **The underworld.** Not central to this novella. Referenced only as the place mortals go and Medusa, being mortal, will.

## What mortals know

- Gods exist. They are feared, propitiated, occasionally loved.
- Prayer and sacrifice work, unevenly.
- Prophecy is real but obscure.
- Fate (*moira*) sits above the gods. Even Zeus bends.

## What gods can do

- Change shape, including into humans, animals, weather.
- Bless and curse. Curses tend toward the ironic — an inversion of what the cursed most valued.
- Speak through oracles, dreams, omens (birds, entrails, the rustle of leaves at Dodona).
- Bleed *ichor*, not blood. Feel pain. Do not die.

## What gods cannot do

- Undo another god's working without consent or contest.
- Overturn fate.
- Love mortals without consequence to those mortals.

## Technology & daily life

- Weaving is sacred and domestic both. Athena's domain.
- Ships are small, single-sailed, hugging coastlines.
- Writing exists but is rare — Medusa can read Linear letters carved on temple dedications; she does not write long.
- Light after dark is lamp, hearth, moon. Interiors are close and smoky.

## Geography we use

- **Sarpedon** — rocky island, Medusa's birthplace, home of the Gorgons in some sources. We use it.
- **Athens** — the temple of Athena. Pre-classical, small, severe.
- **The far west** — the cave beyond the known map, past the garden of the Hesperides, where the Gorgons settle after the curse.
- **Seriphos** — Perseus's home; off-page.
