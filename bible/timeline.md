# Timeline

Years are approximate and internal. Medusa's age anchors the chronology.

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| Medusa age 0 | Born on Sarpedon to Phorcys and Ceto. Stheno and Euryale, already adult and immortal, receive her. |
| 6 | First memory we use: a storm, Euryale carrying her up from the shore. |
| 12 | Noticed by a travelling priest; proposed for Athena's temple. |
| 14 | Taken to Athens. Enters the temple. |
| 15-17 | Years at the temple. Loves Athena. Learns the rites. |
| 18 | The night in the temple. The curse. Carried west by her sisters. |
| 18-? | Years at the cave. Heroes arrive, one by one. The garden grows. |
| Last year | Perseus. |

## Internal chronology of the novella

The novella is told from the cave, in the days before Perseus arrives. Medusa is writing — or remembering, or saying aloud to her sisters — in the present. The flashbacks fill in the history above. The present-tense frame is the waiting.

## Off-page events we honour

- Perseus's conception, infancy, the chest at sea, Seriphos, Polydectes's feast, the oath — all known, none dramatised. Perseus arrives already equipped.
- The birth of Pegasus and Chrysaor from Medusa's neck: the novella ends at the blade. The births are epilogue or omitted. Decide before Act III is drafted. (See `notes/open-questions.md`.)
