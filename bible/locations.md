# Locations

## Sarpedon

The island of Medusa's childhood. Steep, sun-bleached, scrubbed by wind. Goats, olive, thyme. A small fishing settlement on the leeward side; the Gorgons' household sits above it, built into the rock. Medusa remembers the smell of brine and hot stone.

## The temple of Athena, Athens

Pre-classical. Small, severe, unpainted. Olive-wood columns, a cella with the goddess's image in wood, the olive tree outside. The priestesses' quarters are a single long room behind the cella. Lamps on bronze stands. The floor is cool even in summer.

Details that matter:

- The altar is in the cella, not outside.
- The olive tree outside is sacred and old.
- The sea is visible from the steps, below and to the west.

## The cave

At the edge of the known world, beyond the garden of the Hesperides. The Gorgons' home after the curse. A limestone cave that opens onto a beach of grey pebbles. Inside: a hearth, three beds, the bones of heroes arranged by Stheno like a mother arranging her daughter's dowry. Outside: the statues, Medusa's garden of men.

The cave faces west. Medusa watches the sun drown in it every evening.

## Seriphos

Off-page. Perseus's home. Mentioned once, perhaps, as the place the young man came from.

## The sea

Not a location; a character. Poseidon's domain. The sound of it under every scene on Sarpedon and at the cave.
