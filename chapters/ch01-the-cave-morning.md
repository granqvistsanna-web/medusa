## I wake

The serpents wake before I do. One of them drinks from the bowl beside my bed. The bowl is clay. The water in it is from the spring at the back of the cave. The serpent dips its head and raises it and dips it again. Water runs down my shoulder.

I open my eyes.

The cave is grey. The light comes from the mouth of the cave and from the hole in the roof. The air smells of salt and of the fire Stheno lit before she went out. I do not know what hour it is. I have not known what hour it is for many years. The sun comes up behind the cliff and sets in the sea to the west. The light in the cave is eastern in the morning and western in the evening. That is all we need.

## What Euryale sings

Euryale is sitting by the fire. She is combing out her hair with her fingers. The serpents in her hair are older than mine and slower. They do not mind being combed. She is singing.

The song is not about us. It is about a girl who waited on a beach for a ship. The ship did not come. The girl walked into the sea. The song does not say what happened to her. The song ends at the step she took from the sand into the water.

Euryale sings this song often. She does not sing it for me. She sings it for herself, or for someone I did not know.

When she sees I am awake she stops.

"Good morning," she says.

"Good morning," I say.

She does not start again.

## Stheno on the beach

I go to the mouth of the cave. The beach is grey stones. The sea is the colour it is at this hour, which has no name.

Stheno is at the edge of the water. She has a knife in her right hand, pointed down. She is not using it. She is looking at the sea. Her wings are folded. The tide moves around her ankles. The tide moves in and the tide moves out.

I do not call to her. She knows I am there.

I go back inside.

## What I know

I know that someone is coming. I will not say how I know. I have had years to learn.

I do not tell Euryale. I do not tell Stheno. They know. Euryale has stopped singing. Stheno is on the beach with a knife she will not use.

We do not say it to each other.

## The sound

I sit by the fire. Euryale gives me bread and olives and water. The bread is from the morning before. The water is cold.

A sound comes from the shore.

It is not the sound of the sea and it is not the sound of a boat. It is above the beach and also on the beach. It is a sound I have heard before in my life but not often, and not from a shore.

Euryale looks up from the fire. She does not speak.

I put the bread down.
